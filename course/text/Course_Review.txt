FOUNDATIONS · PROBABILITY


Probability rules and the conditional trap
COMPLEMENT
P(not A) = 1 − P(A)
                                       P(A | B) ≠ P(B | A)
ADDITION
                                       A p-value is P(data this extreme | H₀), not the probability that H₀
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B)
                                       is true.
CONDITIONAL
                                       Hypothetical screening test: 1% prevalence, 90% sensitivity, 90%
P(A | B) = P(A and B) / P(B)
                                       specificity. Only about 8% of positive results are true positives.
INDEPENDENCE
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)
                                       Regression predicts E[Y | X]: a mean conditional on X.
BAYES
P(A | B) = P(B | A) × P(A) / P(B)
