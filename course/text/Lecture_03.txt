The Line Graph – Tracking Continuity

  Lines imply continuity. They suggest that values between
  measured points exist and are changing.
  •    Default for time-series data (Jan → Dec)
  •    Emphasize on the changes along the timeline
  •    Avoid "Spaghetti Charts": 15 lines in 15 colors is unreadable.
  •    Highlight the story line; grey out the rest.

The scatter plot, and what to check before you believe it
  OVERPLOTTING
  Dots land on top of each other. The dense centre may hold ten times more firms than it appears to. Fix with transparency or smaller
  markers.


  ONE CLOUD OR TWO
  Clusters almost always mean a third variable is doing the work. Colour by industry and look again before you read the overall shape.



  WHAT DRIVES THE LINE
  Cover the far right of the plot with your hand. Does the trendline still look like that? If not, a few firms are writing your conclusion.



  LOG OR RAW
  Two right-skewed variables give you a dense corner and a few distant dots. Log both axes first, then look at it again.
