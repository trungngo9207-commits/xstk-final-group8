     The Addition rule
     Derived from the third Kolmogorov axiom by decomposing A ∪ B into disjoint parts




       THE ADDITION RULE
       P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ 𝐁)
       The subtracted term is the region counted twice: adding
       the two shares counts every case in the intersection
       once for A and again for B.
       • The third axiom applies only to disjoint events
       • For overlapping events the intersection must be removed once
       • P(A) + P(B) is correct only when the intersection is empty
