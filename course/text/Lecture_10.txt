MATCHED PAIR COMPARISONS


Matched Pairs Definition

• In comparing two treatments, it is
  desirable that the experimental units or
  subjects be as alike as possible, so that
  a difference in responses between the
  groups can be attributed to differences
  in treatments.
• But large variability can occur in the
  measurements.
• The procedure (sampling by matched
  pairs) consists of choosing units in pairs
  or blocks so that the units in each block
  are similar and those in different blocks
  are dissimilar.

The Role of Randomization




        Random assignment makes the two groups alike, on average, in everything except the
          treatment. So that the difference between groups can be read as the effect of the
                                             treatment.

  Where assignment cannot be randomized — the choice of exchange, a firm's industry, a customer's own decision to
      join a loyalty programme — a two-sample test still tells whether the groups differ. But it cannot say why.
